# Social Media

Use these English templates for a direct message, a public post or a comment addressed to a relevant account. Choose a topic and recipient, replace the account and location placeholders, and add a personal sentence. Each message includes a reference matched to its topic and Instagram statements from the centre-right EPP and from Greens/EFA and The Left MEPs. For a private message, sign with your name or organisation.

“Local” means the country where you live: contact its media, relevant institutions or national MPs. The EU MP templates are for Members of the European Parliament. For longer messages with figures and references, use the [letters](messages/index.md).

| Recipient | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| Local Media | Investigate, verify and report for local audiences. |
| Local Institution | Use diplomatic or monitoring channels to seek safeguards and follow-up. |
| Local MP | Question their own government and seek national parliamentary scrutiny. |
| EU MP | Question the Commission and scrutinize accession progress and EU action. |
