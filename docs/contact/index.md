# Contact

One page per edition. Pick a topic, then a goal — it's used for both steps below:

1. **Share first message** — a short post, comment or DM.
2. **Share details** — the full letter, for when they ask for more.

## Choose an edition

- **[English (EU)](eu.md)** — contact EU institutions and MEPs.
- **[English (USA)](us.md)** — contact Congress and the US State Department.
- **[English (UK)](gb.md)** — contact the UK Parliament and the FCDO.
