# Social Media — United States

A United States edition of the [Social Media](index.md) templates. Recipients here are US media, US institutions and Congress — not EU institutions or MEPs. Pick a topic and recipient below, fill in the account and location placeholders, and add a personal sentence; sign private messages with your name.

| Recipient | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| US Media | Investigate, verify and report for US audiences, including Albanian-American communities. |
| US Institution | Use State Department or human-rights channels to seek safeguards and follow-up. |
| US Representative | Question their own position and seek House scrutiny of the US response. |
| US Senator | Question the State Department and seek Senate Foreign Relations Committee scrutiny. |
