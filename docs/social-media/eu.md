# Social Media (EU edition)

The EU edition of [Social Media](index.md): recipients here are EU institutions and MEPs, plus your own country's local media, institutions and MPs. Pick a topic and recipient below, fill in the account and location placeholders, and add a personal sentence; sign private messages with your name. “Local” means your own country — contact its media, institutions or MPs there. For longer messages with figures and references, use the [letters](../messages/eu.md) instead.

In the United States, contact Congress and the State Department rather than EU institutions — use the [English (USA)](us.md) edition. In the United Kingdom, contact Parliament and the FCDO instead — use the [English (UK)](gb.md) edition.

| Recipient | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| Local Media | Investigate, verify and report for local audiences. |
| Local Institution | Use diplomatic or monitoring channels to seek safeguards and follow-up. |
| Local MP | Question their own government and seek national parliamentary scrutiny. |
| EU MP | Question the Commission and scrutinize accession progress and EU action. |
