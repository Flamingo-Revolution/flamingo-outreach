# Social Media — United Kingdom

A United Kingdom edition of the [Social Media](index.md) templates. Recipients here are UK media, UK institutions and Parliament — not EU institutions or MEPs, which the UK left in 2020. Pick a topic and recipient below, fill in the account and location placeholders, and add a personal sentence; sign private messages with your name.

| Recipient | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| UK Media | Investigate, verify and report for UK audiences, including Albanian communities in the UK. |
| UK Institution | Use FCDO or human-rights channels to seek safeguards and follow-up. |
| UK MP | Question the Foreign Secretary and seek Commons scrutiny of the UK response. |
| UK Peer | Raise the issue in the Lords and seek scrutiny of the UK response. |
