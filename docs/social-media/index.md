# Social Media

A public post, comment or direct message is quick to send and still counts: a newsroom, an institution or an elected representative who sees the same question arrive from several people in their own country or constituency is more likely to look into it than from one unprompted email. Pick the edition that matches your recipient, fill in the account and location placeholders, and add a personal sentence; sign private messages with your name. For longer messages with figures and references, use the [Letters](../messages/index.md) instead.

Every edition covers the same topics — civil rights, violence against youth, corruption, environment, press freedom, detention — for four kinds of recipient:

| Recipient | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| Local Media | Investigate, verify and report for local audiences. |
| Local Institution | Use diplomatic or monitoring channels to seek safeguards and follow-up. |
| Local MP | Question their own government and seek national parliamentary scrutiny. |
| EU MP | Question the Commission and scrutinize accession progress and EU action. |
