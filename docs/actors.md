Civil-society actors — NGOs, diaspora groups, student and press-freedom organizations, and other non-institutional, non-party groups — already part of the Flamingo Revolution movement, not outreach targets being evaluated for a response. Every actor listed here is included because they are participating; the **evidence link** below is a citation for that participation, not a test of it. Actors are also the people and groups who research and complete the other outreach records on this site: an institution, party or media entry can be marked **Assigned To** an actor who is following up on it, and credited to a **Contributor** who entered or last updated it. Both fields are optional and may be blank until someone takes on that entry. An actor can also set a **country of interest** — the institutions recorded there are listed on the actor's own entry below as suggested outreach targets. See the [network](network.md) of actors, actions and parent groups, or select a country on the map to inspect its actors.

## Download the data

[Download the CSV](assets/data/actors.csv) or [update the shared workbook](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/19qp5A7qB5DwoKEF6WlYKy_GSWNOxTgMX/edit?usp=sharing). This is a snapshot, so responses may change between checks. The [action log](assets/data/actions.csv) cross-references these actors with the [actions](actions/index.md) they have taken.

**Read the status:** every actor on this page is part of the movement by virtue of being listed. A green entry has a citable link to evidence of its involvement; a red entry does not have one on file yet — that is a gap in this tracker's citations, not a sign the actor is less active or less involved. Being named in a recorded **action** (see the [network](network.md)) already counts as that evidence: an actor does not need a separate link of its own if the joint action it took part in already cites one — this page fills that link in automatically. A recorded entry does not imply support for any particular demand beyond what its own evidence shows. Some entries also carry a separate **Response Link**, recorded when the actor responded directly to this project's outreach.
