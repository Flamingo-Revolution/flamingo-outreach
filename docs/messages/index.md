# Letters

A longer, sourced email is what moves a newsroom to actually investigate, or gives an institution or representative something concrete to act on — figures, dates and links they can check and cite, not just a request for attention. Pick the edition that matches your recipient, add a few words of your own, and send it in your name. For something quicker to post or comment, use [Social Media](../social-media/index.md) instead.

Every edition has templates for four kinds of recipient: local media, local institutions, your own MPs, and EU institutions, Congress or Parliament depending on the edition.

## Choose an edition

- **[English (EU)](eu.md)** — contact EU institutions, the European Parliament and the Commission.
- **[English (USA)](us.md)** — contact Congress and the US State Department.
- **[English (UK)](gb.md)** — contact the UK Parliament and the FCDO.
- Or pick your own language below — these translate the EU edition for your own country's media, institutions and MPs.
