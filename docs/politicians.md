Individual politicians, MEPs, party figures and political groups who have already spoken, voted, visited or otherwise engaged on Albania's protests — not outreach targets being evaluated for a response. Every entry here is included because of a documented engagement; the **evidence link(s)** below cite that engagement, not test it. A listed entry does not imply support for every demand of the protests beyond what its own evidence shows, and a missing link does not establish that a figure was inactive. National MPs and parliamentary groups contacted directly by this project are tracked separately on the [Parties & groups](parties.md) page.

## Download the data

[Download the CSV](assets/data/politicians.csv) or [update the shared workbook](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/19qp5A7qB5DwoKEF6WlYKy_GSWNOxTgMX/edit?usp=sharing). This is a snapshot, so new engagements may be added between checks.

**Read the status:** every politician on this page is included because a documented engagement is on file. A green entry has a citable link to evidence of that engagement; a red entry does not have one on file yet — that is a gap in this tracker's citations, not a sign that the engagement did not happen. A recorded entry does not imply support for any particular demand beyond what its own evidence shows.
